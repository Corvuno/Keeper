# Steering by the last message — resolved, watch for it

**What happened.** The Keeper took each newest remark as the whole picture: one remark became a
rule for everything, earlier wishes dropped out of view, and card rounds swung to the opposite
extreme after one comment.
**What the owner expected.** An overview: where do we stand, is this the right thing to do. "You
latch on to the last thing I said, forgetting everything."
**Cause.** Nothing held the overview. The backlog held tasks and the records held decisions, but
no page said where the product stands.
**What was changed.** `state.md` in every product, read first; "Keep the overview" in the
Keeper's description; a comment on one thing changes that thing only.
