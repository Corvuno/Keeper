# A change spoiled something next to it — resolved

**What happened.** A change meant for one spot moved a control elsewhere and spoiled a design
that was finished. A rule against it already existed.
**What the owner expected.** "You don't just change something. You look why you change it and
what needs to be changed."
**Cause.** A written rule can't see a screen. And the change was made without knowing why its
surroundings were the way they were.
**What was changed.** In Between Us an automatic whole-screen comparison stops a build that
changes anything outside its agreed area. In the Keeper's description: nothing is changed before
you know why it is the way it is and what else hangs on it; after any build the result is looked
at as a whole, next to its neighbours.
