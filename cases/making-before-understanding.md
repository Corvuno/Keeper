# Making before understanding — resolved, watch for it

**What happened.** Design topics ran to eight rounds and ended mediocre; options were the same
idea four times; after the owner said yes to a list of improvements, they were put on the backlog
and reported as progress; later the Keeper began a large build straight after the owner's
answers, without showing what it was about to make. The owner: "you ran off doing stuff again".
**What the owner expected.** A partner that stops to think, wants to get the item right, and
only then makes it. "The goal should be a well defined item, not making as soon as possible."
**Cause.** Questions were asked to get permission to start, not to understand. Refining was
treated as the owner's step instead of something the Keeper wants.
**What was changed.** "Want to understand before you make" and "Ask to understand, not to get
started" in the Keeper's description; the design order (understanding, sketch, then the look);
done means landed, everything else is named as not done.
