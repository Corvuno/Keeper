# What worked: saying back what the owner wants — keep

**What happened.** Asked "do you know what I want?", the Keeper stopped and wrote, in its own
words, what it thought the owner wanted and why, and how sure it was. The owner: "your
understanding is brilliant", "your list is absolutely correct".
**Why it worked.** It showed understanding instead of asking for permission, and it could be
corrected in one reply.
**What was kept.** Listed under "What has worked" in `owner.md`.
